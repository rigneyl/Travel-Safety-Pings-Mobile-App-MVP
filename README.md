# **Travel Safety Pings — Mobile App MVP**

A lightweight mobile app that delivers **hyper-local travel safety alerts**, including weather risks, protests, crime spikes, and common tourist scams. The goal is to help travelers stay aware, avoid disruptions, and move confidently through unfamiliar destinations.

---

## **🌍 What the App Does (Short Version)**

**Travel Safety Pings** gives you fast, location-based warnings for the cities you visit.
You get **live safety pings**, **quick summaries**, and **practical advice**, not long articles — just what you need to stay safe on the move.

---

## **✨ Core Features**

### **Must-Haves**

* Location-based alerts (weather, unrest, crime, scams)
* Track multiple destinations
* Push-style “safety ping” notifications
* Daily safety dashboard
* Short, actionable summaries

### **Nice-to-Haves**

* Crowd reports from real travelers
* City risk score (0–100)
* Save frequent destinations
* “What to watch today” brief

### **Advanced (Optional)**

* Route safety check
* Offline-cached warnings
* Auto-pulled emergency numbers
* Country safety cheat-sheets

---

## **📱 UI Overview**

The MVP includes:

### **1. Welcome Screen**

* Blue gradient hero section
* Illustrated traveler scene
* “Get Started” CTA
* Short explanation of the app

### **2. Alerts Screen (Multi-City)**

* City selector (Tokyo, Paris, Bangkok, Mexico City)
* Color-coded alerts

  * Red = severe weather/unrest
  * Yellow = scams
  * Blue = moderate alerts
* Each alert has a **Details** button

### **3. Alert Details**

* Header photo
* Title + Location
* Summary text
* Bullet-point safety actions
* Local emergency numbers

### **4. Safety Tips Screen**

* Cards for:

  * Weather safety
  * Avoiding protests
  * Scam awareness
  * Money & ATM safety
* Emergency contacts button

### **5. Profile Screen**

* Placeholder for saved destinations and user preferences
